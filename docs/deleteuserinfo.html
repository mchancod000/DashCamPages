Data Deletion

App: RoadReplay  ·  Last updated: May 26, 2026

Delete your account from within the app

The easiest way to delete your data is directly from the app. Open RoadReplay on your device and follow these steps:

  1. Tap the Settings icon
  2. Scroll down to Delete Account and All Data
  3. Tap the button and confirm when prompted

This will immediately and permanently:

  • Delete all of your uploaded cloud clips from our servers
  • Delete your account and sign-in information
  • Remove all associated metadata, session data, and entitlement records from our systems
Local recordings saved on your device are not affected — those are stored only on your phone and must be deleted through your device's gallery or file manager.

Can't access the app?

If you no longer have access to the app, email us at the address below with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include the Google account email address associated with your RoadReplay account. We will manually remove your data within 30 days.
