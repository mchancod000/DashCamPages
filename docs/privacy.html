Privacy Policy

App: RoadReplay  ·  Last updated: May 26, 2026

What RoadReplay does

RoadReplay is a dashcam app. It records video and audio to your device while you drive. A paid premium subscription adds Google sign-in and cloud backup so your clips can be stored, restored, and managed across installs.

Free tier

  • Records video and audio locally
  • Clips stay on your device
  • Optional live speed display
  • No account, no sign-in
  • No data leaves your device

Premium tier

  • Everything in Free, plus:
  • Google Sign-In
  • Cloud backup and restore
  • Cloud clip browser (view, protect, delete)
  • Loop recording mode
  • 25 GB cloud storage cap
  • 14-day rolling retention

How we use your data

We use your data only to operate the app:

  • Record and save dashcam clips on your device
  • Display optional live speed information
  • Authenticate you with Google and verify your subscription
  • Upload, list, protect, restore, preview, and delete cloud clips
  • Enforce storage limits, retention policies, and per-device access rules
  • Diagnose and resolve service failures through backend logs

Where data is stored

Free tier: All recordings stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded unless you export it yourself.

Premium tier: Clips you back up are uploaded to our cloud backend (Cloudflare) and cloud object storage (Backblaze B2). Account, session, entitlement, and clip metadata is stored in a backend database to run the service. Unprotected clips are automatically deleted after 14 days or when you exceed the 25 GB storage cap. Protected clips are exempt from automatic retention removal.

Third-party services

The app relies on these providers. Each receives only the data needed to deliver its part of the service:

  • Google Sign-In — identity and OAuth token for authentication
  • Google Play — subscription purchase verification
  • Cloudflare — backend API hosting and database
  • Backblaze B2 — cloud object storage for clip files

Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. We do not use advertising IDs or any ad network. Data is shared only with the service providers above, and only to the extent necessary to deliver sign-in, subscription verification, backend operations, and cloud storage.

Your controls

  • Delete local recordings — delete clips directly from the app or your device gallery
  • Delete cloud clips — delete individual clips from the cloud clips screen inside the app
  • Sign out of Google — available in Settings; stops cloud access from this device
  • Delete your account and all data — Settings → Delete Account and All Data permanently removes your cloud account, all uploaded clips, and all associated records from our servers. Local recordings on your device are not affected.
  • Cancel subscription — managed through Google Play
Automatic retention: unprotected cloud clips may also be removed after 14 days or when your storage exceeds the 25 GB cap, regardless of cancellation or inactivity, depending on your subscription state and storage policies.

Diagnostics

The app writes a local diagnostics log to your device to help troubleshoot recording and upload issues. This log is never automatically transmitted. You can share it manually via Settings → Share Diagnostics Log if you contact us for support. The backend also records operational error logs and audit events to investigate service failures.

Children

RoadReplay is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
