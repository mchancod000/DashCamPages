Privacy Policy

App: DashCam Simple
Last updated: 2025-08-22

What this app does

Simple DashCam records video and audio locally on your device using your camera and microphone.

Data collection

  • No personal data is sent to our servers. Recordings are saved locally (e.g., Movies/Dashcam).
  • We do not collect analytics, advertising IDs, or location.

Permissions

  • Camera – capture video.
  • Microphone – capture audio.
  • Notifications – foreground service status (Android).

Third-party sharing

We do not share your data with third parties.

Data retention & control

You can delete recordings at any time from within the app or your device’s file manager.
