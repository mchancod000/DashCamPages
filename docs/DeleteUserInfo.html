RoadReplay — Data Deletion

Delete your account from within the app

Open the RoadReplay app on your device, then go to Settings → Delete Account and All Data.

Tapping this button will immediately and permanently:

  • Delete all of your uploaded cloud clips from our servers
  • Delete your account and sign-in information
  • Remove all associated metadata from our systems

Your local recordings stored on your device are not affected and must be deleted manually through your device's gallery or file manager.

This action cannot be undone.
